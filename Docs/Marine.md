# Marine life

104 procedural sea creatures and plants under `/BiomeAutoMaterials/Marine`, generated by the kit's own scripts
(`Tools/marine/marine_life.py`, `Tools/marine/marine_fauna.py`), released under CC0. There are no image textures: colour
and pattern come from vertex colours, UVs and the materials, so the whole set weighs about 11 MB as source.

## What there is

- **Stony and soft corals**: brain, boulder, table, staghorn, elkhorn, pillar, lettuce, finger and fire coral; sea fans,
  sea plumes, sea rods and sea whips.
- **Sponges and the like**: barrel, tube, rope and encrusting sponges, anemones, feather-duster worms.
- **Plants and algae**: turtle grass, manatee grass, kelp, bull kelp, wrack, sea lettuce, red algae and the Caribbean
  calcareous algae (Halimeda, Padina, Caulerpa, Udotea, shaving brush, Dictyota), floating sargassum rafts.
- **Bottom life**: queen conch, starfish, sand dollars, long-spined urchins, sea cucumbers, mussel beds, shells,
  ghost crab, crab, lobster, octopus, moray, flounder.
- **Rock**: reef rock, boulders, cobbles, pinnacles and skerries (the last two with collision, so boats stop on them).
- **Fish**: sardines, herring, grunts, snappers, tangs, angelfish, butterflyfish, sergeant majors, parrotfish, groupers,
  hogfish, squirrelfish, spadefish, porkfish, jacks, goatfish, cowfish, trumpetfish, cod, lionfish, pufferfish, queen
  triggerfish, tuna, tarpon, barracuda, mahi-mahi, swordfish, blue marlin.
- **Sharks, rays and the large ones**: reef, bull, blacktip, oceanic whitetip, tiger, hammerhead, nurse and great white
  sharks; eagle ray, manta, stingray; green turtle; seal, dolphin, orca, humpback whale.
- **Jellyfish**: moon jelly, box jelly, sea nettle, lion's mane, Portuguese man o' war.

All are real size (1 unit = 1 cm). Colours are linear albedos as they look in shallow water: reef corals are mostly
browns, ochres and olives from their algae, with the bright ones the exception, as on a real reef.

## Materials

- **`M_SeaLife`** (corals, plants, rock, bottom life). Vertex colour R is how freely a vertex sways, G the cavity
  shade, B the share of the species' second colour, A the polyp pattern. Close to, rock and stony coral get a
  triplanar grain from the biome library's `rock_face` photograph. Plants sway in the surge (world position offset):
  to and fro, neighbours together, tips lagging behind roots. Rock that reaches the surface (`Tidal`) has a black wet
  band and weed at sea level (world Z 0).
- **`M_SeaCreature`** (everything that swims) and **`M_SeaJelly`** (translucent). Patterns are drawn from UV0 (U along
  the body, V round it): bands, stripes, spots, scales, coloured heads, eyes, gill slits, a turtle's scutes, an orca's
  and a great white's markings. The swimming is vertex animation in the mesh's own frame: a fish's tail wave with
  bursts and glides, whale flukes, ray wings, turtle flippers, a jellyfish's pulse, a moray breathing, an octopus's arms,
  and a pufferfish blowing up (`Puff`). No skeleton and no Blueprint: they swim in the editor viewport.
- **Shoals**: small fish (`Travel` 1) placed as an instanced mesh swim round an ellipse given by per-instance custom
  data (D0 radius A cm, D1 radius B cm, D2 angular speed rad/s, D3 phase, D4 heading rad), hurry and dawdle together,
  and part round a camera that comes closer than `Shy` cm. Large fish (`Travel` 0) beat their tails in place: move
  them along a spline or from a Blueprint if they should cruise.

Every `MI_Sea_<Species>` instance exposes its colours and motion as parameters.

## Placing it

`Tools/assets/scatter_biome.py` places marine life with the island assets. Each biome names its sea in
`MARINE_OF` (`Tools/assets/catalog.py`): **tropical** coral reef and sea-grass meadows, **warm** turbid or salty coasts,
**temperate** kelp forest and mussel beds, **cold** northern bull kelp and cobbles, **volcanic** black boulders. Roles:

| Role | Depth | What |
|---|---|---|
| `reef` | 1.2-28 m | corals, sponges, kelp, boulders, on slopes up to 42 deg |
| `seabed` | 0.5-14 m | sea grass, algae, shells, urchins, starfish, crabs on gentle ground |
| `fish` | 3-40 m | shoals of 8-40 small fish (3-10 bigger ones), mid-water |

```bash
set BAM_BIOME=TropicalIsland
set BAM_SCATTER_ROLES=reef,seabed,fish
```

then run `Tools/assets/scatter_biome.py` in the editor with a level whose landscape goes below sea level.

## Under water

`ABiomeOcean` blends in `M_BiomeUnderwater` when the view goes below the waves: light from every pixel is lost to the
water in between, per channel (`UnderwaterExtinction`, red within a few metres, blue lasting ~30 m in clear tropical
water), and replaced by the water's own glow (`UnderwaterLight`), which dims with the camera's depth and the sun's
height. Turn it off with `bUnderwaterView`.

## Rebuilding

```bash
python Tools/marine/marine_life.py
python Tools/marine/marine_fauna.py
```

writes the meshes to `Build/Marine`; then run `Tools/marine/build_marine_assets.py` in the editor (after
`Tools/build_materials.py`, whose `rock_face` texture gives the grain).
